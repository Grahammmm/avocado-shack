## What a real owner will hit first
1. **He texts a product with a price and gets "Name: Not read yet … Send the product photo or its name."** Example: "add persimmins fuyu $3.50 a lb", or the same in ALL CAPS, as a voice run-on, or as "add organic local strawberries best ever $6 a basket". The name is in his text, but a price in the same clause throws the whole clause away (F2). If he puts the name after a comma, he gets an interview instead of a listing (F3).
2. **He chats while the listing is waiting, and his words land in the public description.** "no wait", "what time do we close sunday", "looks good yes please!" and a mistyped "yes 0846x" are each appended to the description and the website card (see `cards/approve-yes-please-t2.png`). His next correct "yes <code>" publishes that text (F1, S1). The "this is from miners" paste is the same bug.
3. **He changes the price in plain words and is refused.** "make it $4", "actually make it 4 bucks", "wait $4.50" and "ok make it $4" all get "I can't use that wording" or "Give the selling price separately" (F4).
4. **Answering "What price should it sell for?" after a photo gets "That's already how it is, so nothing changed."** The price was never set (F5).
5. **Text-only product messages are slow:** 14 to 23 s each while local Qwen reads the price (F14). That comes on top of the photo and picture delays HASS-LIVE-FIX-2 is measuring.

## Top 10 problems (with evidence)
1. **F1 (S1): owner chatter is pasted into the public description, then published by the next yes.** Repro: `edit-no-wait`, `edit-unrelated-question-mid-draft`, `approve-yes-please`, `approve-typo-code`, `edit-source-miners`. Example: T2 "looks good yes please!" gives `Description: SUPERGRIP GLUE comes in … glue. looks good yes please!.`, and the rendered card shows it (`cards/approve-yes-please-t2.png`, `cards/edit-no-wait-t2.png`, `cards/edit-source-miners-t2.png`). Cause: in the simple-intake answer path (`dispatch._simple_answer` → `intake_simple.edit`), any text that is not a known edit becomes description words. The old-style interpreter is never asked, and no "is this a listing instruction?" check runs. HASS-LIVE-FIX-2 fixes the "miners" instance; the general rule needs these five cases as acceptance.
2. **F2 (S2): a text-only product with a price loses its name.** Repro: `intake-typo-persimmins`, `intake-all-caps`, `intake-voice-runon`, `intake-claims-food`, `intake-extra-units`, `intake-price-letter-o`, `price-huge`, plus all 28 text-opener recordings. Cause: `hass/skills/item_intake.py caption_answers()` (line ~151) drops the WHOLE clause from the public words whenever the answer reader found any money/stock field and the clause has `$` or a commerce word. "add persimmins fuyu $3.50 a lb" is one clause, so the public text is empty and `simple` is True. `cheap()` → `_simple_answer` then never asks for the name. Sending the name alone later does work (`edit-rename`, text opener: "call it Fuyu Persimmons" drafts with a generated illustration).
3. **F3 (S2): text intake without a photo falls into interviews or stale refusals instead of one listing.** "add fuyu persimmons, $3.50/lb" gets "What brand and short description should I use?". "got pomegranates in, three fifty a pound" gets "Should I add them as a new fruit, and what should I call them". "add fuyu persimmons $3.50/lb and also pomegranates $2 each" and "add mangos for -2 dollars" get **"The website doesn't show prices … send it without the price?"**, which contradicts the priced listing flow. The old-style interpreter prompt still carries the catalog-only, no-prices rule and asks interview questions.
4. **F4 (S2): plain price edits are refused.** Repro: `edit-price-plain`, `edit-contradiction`, `edit-unrelated-question-mid-draft` T3, and `approve-old-draft-after-edit` (the refusal stands, so the old $2 listing is what the yes publishes; correct, but the owner believes he asked for $4). Cause: `intake_simple.edit` only takes the bare "$2 each" form; "make it $4" / "4 bucks" / "wait $4.50" / "no $3" hit the "can't use that wording" guard.
5. **F5 (S2): the answer to Hass's own price question is dropped.** `intake-photo-then-two-messages-fast`: persimmon photo → "What price should it sell for?" → "$3.50 a pound" → "That's already how it is, so nothing changed." Then "from Etto" gets the same reply. No draft is ever made.
6. **F6 (S2): one product at a time, and no edits after publishing.** `approve-two-drafts-old-code`: while the glue draft waits, "raspberry jam $7" plus a jam photo gets "I couldn't make that change". `approve-then-change-price`: after publishing, "make the glue $4" starts a NEW nameless listing ("Name: Not read yet, Price: $4.00") instead of a price edit of the published product. If he then sends a photo, that is a duplicate-product risk.
7. **F9 (S2): broken or misleading public text from the label path.** Candle: `Name: Lavender Honey` (no "candle", so a non-food item reads like honey) and "Lavender Honey is an with a visible wick." (`cards/intake-nonfood-candle-t1.png`). Jam: "The label lists a net weight of." (`cards/intake-jam-organic-t1.png`). Also ALL-CAPS label names ("SUPERGRIP GLUE"), double spaces in the website description ("rectangular  container"), and Brand flipping between "Supergrip" and "Producer not identified" across runs.
8. **F7 (S2): two photos in one message breaks the one-reply flow.** `intake-two-photos`: 3 replies, a "Photo 1 out of 2: Is this photo for a product in the shop, a new product, or the home page?" interview per photo, and "I did not read the caption". Front and back of one product is a normal owner habit.
9. **F10 (S2): till reads only work for exact phrasings.** "how many avacados" and "whats low" go to the interpreter, which answers "I don't know how many … I'd need a current count", although the cache has Hass avocado: 40. "are eggs in stock?" gets "The closest is Eggs, dozen: 0 in the till" with no plain yes/no.
10. **F11 (S3): help and status confuse.** `help` is about 90 lines and includes internal capability jargon ("private test supported; eligible for live approval; registered; owners"). Its first line says `yes - go ahead with the change waiting for you`, but a bare yes is refused because approvals need the code. `status` says "I still need your shelf check" without saying how. "shelf" gets "Which shelf would you like to update, and what should it say?". "stopp changes" gets "There are no changes waiting to cancel … say undo" and is never steered to "stop changes".

## Harness challenges met and fixed (in the harness PR, not product code)
- No full-feature harness existed: `fixture_chat.py` and the test `Chat` stub the router to `item.add`. `run_chaos.py` builds the production registry, router, till desk, label/answer readers and previewer the way `host.make_worker` does, all on throwaway state.
- The previewer turns off its pinned Playwright auto-detection when an explicit `env` is passed, so the harness lets it detect Playwright and sets only `NODE`.
- Draft codes hash the base commit and the label-reply key order, so replay must start from the same full-site tree, and tapes keep the reply key order. (The first tapes were saved with sorted keys, so the replayed codes differ from the recorded ones, e.g. 0846 vs f685; the behaviour is identical.)
- Command-guard refusals on this card (recorded on the card): one inline `python3 -c` probe and one `rm -rf`. Neither was retried.
