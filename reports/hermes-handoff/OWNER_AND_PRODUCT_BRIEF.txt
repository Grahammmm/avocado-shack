AVOCADO SHACK — CURRENT BUSINESS CONTEXT FOR HERMES

Michael Wolfe owns Avocado Shack, a produce and specialty food market in Morro Bay.
The store brand centers on healthy food, seasonal produce, independent makers,
local connections, hospitality, community and gratitude. His newsletters are warm,
conversational and specific about food and the people who grow or make it.

The active phase invites people to browse what they may find in person. Product
availability is not synchronized to stock. There are no prices or online orders.
Show the food well, explain taste and use, identify known makers and their bases,
and help visitors understand why independent producers matter. Preserve source
links and distinguish known product facts from unknown growers or exact varieties.
Some product images were generated from photographed evidence. Preserve logs and
source photos; do not represent generated packaging as independently verified.

Community belongs in the store experience: Michael's newsletter and the North
Coast Hiking Club. No actual next hike or past outings were supplied. Route ideas
are examples around SLO County, not scheduled events. michael/mike email accounts
are not configured by this project; owner@example.com is only a proposed sender.

Future direction discussed with the user: Shopify backend/POS and a custom store
assistant called Hass. The owner wants to manage the store through text, voice
notes and photos. Desired future capabilities include intake, costs/margins,
pricing recommendations, stock/POS sync, supplier messages, morning reports,
spoilage discounts, social content and accounting/invoicing integration. Begin
with owner approval and only grant bounded autonomy as comfort and evidence grow.
These are ambitions, not capabilities connected to the current website.

Weekly boxes, merchandise, payment and fulfillment experiments were explicitly
removed from the active site. Do not bring them back merely because old proposal
or prototype files describe them. The campaign website and political outreach
are a separate project; keep their contacts and messaging separate from the store.
