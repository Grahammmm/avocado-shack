# Site text contrast audit, November 2026

Measured text/background colour pairs the home page (with its catalog) and `/hike` render, against WCAG 2.2 AA (1.4.3: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text). **This is a measurement, not a conformance claim**: failing rows are findings for a later change; no CSS was changed. Non-text contrast, Safari and physical devices are out of scope.

## How this was measured

- Command: `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=<playwright> python3 storefront-mockup/site-contrast-qa.py --write` (and `--check`, the default, which byte-compares).
- `sitegen.publish_files(..., today=2026-11-05)` writes two fixture copies to a scratch folder outside the repository; a loopback static server and headless Chromium measure them. Real templates, runtime renderers and CSS are used; fixtures only supply data.
- Fixture differences from normal content: **site A** is the committed content (no notice, no availability). **Site B** is a deep copy with `store.notice` = "Fixture notice: the Shack opens at noon on Saturday." (until 2026-11-20), `selection_checked_on` 2026-11-05 and six products given an availability word (Available today, Limited, Sold out, and three "Last confirmed" days). `/hike` club events, RSVP counts and service replies are fixtures (`hike-real-events`), answered from the page's own `/api/community` and POST routes; nothing leaves loopback and every other host is blocked.
- Browser: Chromium 156.0.8078.4; viewports 390x844, 1440x900; fixed clock 2026-11-05T20:00:00.000Z (12:00 Pacific, 2026-11-05); Pacific time zone; `document.fonts.ready` awaited; reduced motion.
- Text runs: every nonempty rendered text node, input values and placeholders, `::before`/`::after` generated text and SVG text (computed fill). Foreground is the computed colour with alpha and ancestor opacity applied; background is every ancestor layer composited in painting order over the white canvas (opacity scales each layer; exact for opaque layers). Text over an image or another box is measured from the backing pixels under the text bounds with the text suppressed (worst case reported with the lightest and darkest backing). Large text is 24 px, or 18.666667 px at weight 700 or more.
- Ratios are unrounded WCAG ratios compared to the threshold (shown to 3 decimals; a 4.499 never passes as 4.5).
- "Nearest passing foreground" is an opaque 24-bit colour with the background held fixed, minimising squared Euclidean distance in encoded RGB bytes from the measured foreground (ties: lowest RGB), searched exactly over all 16,777,216 colours. For a selector measured on several backings it must pass on every one.
- Interaction: every kind of link, button, summary and control is measured at default, pointer hover and keyboard focus (one instance per kind, deduplicated by selector and context).
- Limits: hidden/closed content is excluded until revealed (disclosures and dialogs are measured open); the collector is not an axe run; text in disabled controls is listed as exempt; the logo and photographs are images, not text.
