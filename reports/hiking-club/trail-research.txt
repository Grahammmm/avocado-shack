POINT BUCHON — LOS OSOS
https://www.pge.com/en/about/in-your-community/recreational-areas.html
PG&E: 6.6-mile round trip; 300-foot elevation change (not stated as cumulative
ascent). Coon Creek parking lot / Pecho Valley Road, Montaña de Oro State Park.
Trail attendant check-in required; no pets. Check current access before an event.
Moderate difficulty and 3–4 hours are our group-planning estimates, not a PG&E
rating. November 8, 8:45 meeting, 9:00 start and 12:30 return are sample plans.
The original SVG is a stylized illustration. It is not traced trail geometry,
surveyed topography, an elevation profile or a navigation aid.

CASS HILL — CAYUCOS
https://highway1roadtrip.com/things-to-do/cayucos-hiking/
Local tourism guide: 3 miles out and back, 692 feet gain, moderate difficulty.
The mockup rounds ascent to about 690 feet. About 2 hours is a planning estimate.
Other route descriptions give different lengths/gains; confirm the chosen route
and access with Michael before scheduling it. October 2026 is a sample archive
month, not a claim that the club hiked this route.

FISCALINI RANCH BLUFF TRAIL — CAMBRIA
https://www.cambriacsd.org/trails
Cambria CSD: Bluff Trail is 0.9 mile, with boardwalk/gravel and handcrafted benches.
An out-and-back example is therefore 1.8 miles. Easy / mostly level is descriptive;
no unsupported numeric ascent is used. 1–1.5 hours allows stops and conversation.
September 2026 is a sample archive month, not a confirmed club outing.
