# Cass Hill and Fiscalini Ranch — map source readiness (November 2026)

Research only. No map assets were downloaded, generated or changed. These are undated route ideas, not confirmed outings or navigation guidance.

**Researched on:** 4 November 2026 (Pacific; the OSM and Commons replies are stamped 5 November UTC). Checked against avocado-shack main `867e8d0`.

## Scope

- This report lists what the Point Buchon map method (`reports/hiking-club/TRAIL_MAP_SOURCES.txt`, sections PHOTOGRAPH, BASEMAPS, ROUTE, ELEVATION) would need for the two existing route ideas in `hass/content/community.json`: `idea-cass-hill` (lines 30-46) and `idea-fiscalini-bluff` (lines 48-64).
- Existing description facts (from those records, unchanged): Cass Hill, Cayucos, 3 miles out and back, about 690 ft gain, exposed hillside with some steep sections, source Highway 1 Road Trip. Fiscalini, Cambria, **Bluff Trail only**, 1.8 miles out and back, mostly level, boardwalk and gravel, source Cambria CSD. A whole-ranch loop is not this route.
- Newly read on public pages: the two source guides, OSM way metadata, the two USGS basemap service pages, the USGS 3DEP service page, Wikimedia Commons file metadata. Only small metadata lookups were made (way tags and node lists, service descriptions, file licence fields). No dataset, tile, image, terrain sample or export was fetched, and nothing was written under `storefront-mockup/assets/trails`.
- Source availability is not a finished map, a verified track, access clearance or permission to publish.

## Common rights (apply to both routes)

- **OpenStreetMap:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (page read, HTTP 200). Credit must be visible and linked. A derived route GeoJSON is a derived database and needs the same credit and licence note, as for Point Buchon. This does not make the Highway 1 Road Trip or Cambria CSD guide text or any official map share that licence; those are only linked.
- **USGS basemaps and 3DEP:** the service pages (read) carry these credits. Imagery: "USDA, USGS The National Map: Orthoimagery. Data refreshed June, 2024.v1.2". The USGS rights page https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits returned HTTP 403 to this tool, so the public-domain wording was **not re-read** here; the method's own statement ("public-domain CONUS basemap data") stands, and a builder should re-read it in a browser. The Topo credit lists non-USGS inputs (Census, USFS, Natural Earth, NOAA), so "public domain" should be confirmed for the topo layer before it is relied on.

## idea-cass-hill — Cass Hill, Cayucos

Route description from the records: 3 mi out and back, about 690 ft gain (guide says 692 ft), moderate, parking at the end of Cayucos Dr. The Highway 1 Road Trip page (https://highway1roadtrip.com/things-to-do/cayucos-hiking/, read 4 Nov 2026) gives only that summary and prose about views of Cayucos, Whale Rock Reservoir and Morro Rock. It gives **no turnaround point, no trail line, no trail-use permission statement** and no map.

## idea-fiscalini-bluff — Fiscalini Ranch Preserve, Bluff Trail, Cambria

Route description from the records: Bluff Trail only, 1.8 mi out and back, mostly level, boardwalk and gravel. The Cambria CSD page (https://www.cambriacsd.org/trails, read 4 Nov 2026) describes the preserve and its trails in general; the page text this tool received did not show a Bluff Trail length or start/end, so the 1.8 mi figure is the record's own and was checked only against OSM geometry below. Whole-ranch loops and the other named trails (Marine Terrace Trail, SeaClift Trail, Meander Trail, Dolphin Trail and others) are **not** this route.

## Gaps and next step

1. **Cass Hill:** no OSM way named for it; turnaround not stated by the guide; no frame, no sample plan; photo fit unconfirmed. Needs a person to find an official or club-trusted route description (turnaround and access status) before any way IDs are chosen.
2. **Fiscalini:** the Cambria CSD page text gave no Bluff Trail endpoints; compare the nine-way chain with the official trail map first. Rights page for USGS content could not be read by this tool (HTTP 403) and must be re-read in a browser. Photo is ShareAlike.
3. The 3DEP vertical datum and units are carried from the method, not re-read from the service page.
4. Official guide access terms (Cambria CSD preserve rules, any Cayucos land-owner access limits) are separate from data licences and were not assessed.

A later, separately scoped change must acquire and validate assets before any map is built.
