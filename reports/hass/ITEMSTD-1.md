## 6. The ask-Mike flow: summary and conflicts
Full design, in the skill-model shape (five skills with slots, rules, plan, edits, side effects, approval, live class, undo, refusals and fixture scenarios), is `docs/hass/ITEM-STANDARD.md` sections 4.1 to 4.5. Four conflicts found and settled: (1) `resume` is taken (decision C); (2) the spec says Hass "sends nothing unasked", so version 1 asks only inside a conversation Mike started or on a reply he asked for (decision B); (3) **one counting and timing policy** (4.1): all gap questions share the 3 a day, `later` is until 8 am tomorrow, and anything looser (not counting questions Mike starts) is a labelled option for G (decision D), off by default; (4) `later`, `stop asking` and `ask me again` change only private question-control state, so they take effect at once with no approval, while every update to the website keeps the owner's yes, the claims gate and the protected live boundary. A `no` already cancels an open question and counts as a decline. (5) **Who an answer belongs to** (4.2): `gap.fill` binds its subject, field and maker from the open question, not from Mike's words; a producer question belongs to the item, a town or state question always belongs to the maker (so "Petaluma, California" to "Which town is Straus in?" drafts only the maker's place, never asks for an item or the farm again, and "I don't know" to a town question never asks for an item either); eight fixture scenarios (13 to 18, with 14b) pin those paths. All 28 sample lines in that document pass `voice.check` (`itemstd-1/check_voice.py.txt`, section 8); the ITEMSTD-2 and ITEMSTD-5 tests must do the same for every template (the 176 catalog questions already pass).

## 7. Feature backlog: Instagram post for a new item (do not build now)
**What it would do:** when an item is added, or on a command like `post <item>`, render its picture to Instagram sizes in the site's look (ivory, centred, soft shadow): feed portrait 4:5 (1080 x 1350) or square (1080 x 1080), optionally a 3-picture carousel (the item; the maker and town; a serving idea); write a caption in the shop's voice from the house description plus "From <maker> in <town>" (never a price, size or unsaid claim), with the fixed small hashtag set (today `#AvocadoShack` plus one fixed tag per shelf; never from free text); send Mike a preview image. **Mike posts it himself**: Hass never publishes to or sends from a social account. ## 8. Method and limits
* Scripts (`itemstd-1/*.py.txt`, run with `python3`, Pillow 10.2, no numpy): `measure_pictures` (per file: size, format, RIFF chunks, border colour, subject box, margins, shadow), `analyze_pictures` (distributions, robust-deviation score for best and worst, outliers, near-duplicates, sheet), `audit_catalog` (rules over `products.json` and `makers.json`), `build_gaps` (rows, voice-checked questions and the `audit` section with the near-duplicate and generic-name findings), `check_envelope` (the hard and soft picture checks over the 170 pictures, writes `picture-envelope.json`), `check_voice` (every sample line in the standard through `hass/voice.py`: 28 lines, 0 break a rule). * **Machine-valid, not accepted:** best and worst lists are a statistical conformity score (9 measures, robust deviation from the median), not a quality judgment; a human should eyeball the sheet.
