# Avocado Shack sandbox

Read HERMES_HANDOFF.txt and README.txt first. Current source is storefront-mockup/.
The commerce/ prototype and older proposals are historical. BUILD_LEDGER.md is
chronological and its opening objective does not describe the current scope.

- Keep the site private and bound to loopback. Use SSH forwarding for previews.
- Preserve the catalog-only scope: no prices, basket, boxes, merch or checkout.
- Preserve separate newsletter/hiking consent. Email delivery is unconnected.
- Route ideas are undated; create real events only from confirmed owner details.
- Use known producer locations and documented organic claims; never invent origins.
- Do not restore held products without reviewing the recorded manufacturer concern.
- Keep credentials, participant data, runtime and private backups out of Git.
- Keep Mac backup and source-photo provenance. Commit source changes in small units.
- Use isolated fixtures for testing forms; do not pollute the real community database.
- Do not treat historical QA evidence as a fresh check. Record actual checks and limits.
- Report results plainly and keep BUILD_LEDGER.md current at meaningful milestones.
